## 1. Standards roles

| Standard | Role |
|----------|------|
| **IEC 62304** | Software lifecycle processes (planning, requirements, architecture, implementation, verification, release, maintenance) |
| **ISO 14971** | Risk management (hazard analysis, risk evaluation, risk control, residual risk, post-market) |

They are complementary: 14971 identifies hazards; 62304 ensures software process controls mitigate software-contributed hazards.
